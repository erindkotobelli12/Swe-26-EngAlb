# OnboardIQ specification

OnboardIQ converts fragmented onboarding evidence into assessed readiness, findings and accountable next actions.

## Roles

- PMO Coordinator: dashboard, evidence review, actions and audit.
- Employee: own journey, progress, blockers and access confirmation.
- Technical Lead: KT scope, execution evidence and technical validation.

## Core rules

1. Access being approved or provisioned does not imply usable access.
2. Technical validation is separate from training completion.
3. Eva's technical and line-manager reports remain conflicting.
4. Independent readiness requires scope, usable access, required activities and approved technical validation.
5. AI output cannot mutate records and requires human review.
